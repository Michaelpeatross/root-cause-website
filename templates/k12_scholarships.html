Questions

Tap a question to open it.

Can I donate now?

Not yet. The nonprofit is still being set up, so we can't accept gifts. Join the list and we'll email you the day we open.

Is the tax credit guaranteed?

No. It's a new federal program (Internal Revenue Code §25F) planned to start with gifts made on or after January 1, 2027. The rules are still being finalized. Gifts only qualify once the organization is approved and on a participating state's official list.

How much could I save?

Up to $1,700 per person per year, or up to $3,400 for married couples filing jointly. A credit lowers the federal tax you owe, dollar for dollar. You need to owe at least that much tax. Unused credit can carry forward up to 5 years.

Can I give to a specific student?

No. Gifts go to the scholarship fund, not to a particular child.

Who can get a scholarship?

Under the federal program, K-12 students from households earning up to 300% of the area median income who are eligible to attend public school.

Can I also take a charitable deduction?

Not for the part of a gift you claim as a credit. A state tax credit for the same gift also lowers the federal credit. Ask your tax professional.
