The planned federal scholarship tax credit (up to $1,700)

A 2025 federal law created a new credit under Internal Revenue Code §25F for gifts to approved Scholarship Granting Organizations. Treasury's rules are still being finalized, so details may change. Here is how it is expected to work:

Dollar for dollar

A credit reduces the federal income tax you owe by the amount of a qualifying gift, up to $1,700 per person per year. Treasury has said married couples filing jointly may claim up to $3,400.

Starts in 2027

The credit is planned to apply to qualifying cash gifts made on or after January 1, 2027.

State list required

States must opt in. A gift qualifies only if the organization is on a participating state's official SGO list. We are not on any list yet.

Carryforward

The credit is nonrefundable. Any unused amount can be carried forward for up to 5 years.

Example (once eligible)

*Only if you have enough federal income tax liability, and before any other tax effects. You cannot also take a charitable deduction for the part of a gift you claim as a credit. Any state tax credit you claim for the same gift reduces the federal credit.

For families

Under the federal program, scholarships are for K-12 students in households earning up to 300% of area median income who are eligible to enroll in a public school. Applications are not open yet.
