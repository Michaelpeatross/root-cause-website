Questions

Tap a question to open it.

Are you an architect?

No. We are a residential house plans / drafting service, not a licensed architecture firm. We draw complete plan sets for homes. If your city, parish, or lender requires a licensed architect's or engineer's stamp, we'll coordinate that (quoted separately).

Do I need an architect's stamp in Louisiana?

Usually not for a typical one- or two-family home. Louisiana law lets anyone design a single-family home, and under a 2026 state law (Act 881) parishes and cities can't require an architect or engineer stamp on one- or two-family home plans that meet the prescriptive rules of the International Residential Code. Homes outside those rules (for example some high-wind coastal areas, large open spans, or unusual foundations) may still need an engineer. Your lender can also ask for one. Check with your permit office. This isn't legal advice.

What's the difference between the packages?

The Concept gives you a floor plan to react to before you spend more. The Full Construction Set is everything a builder and permit office need. Full Set + 3D adds a 3D model, photo-style renderings, editable CAD files and more revision rounds, so you can see the home before it's built.

How is the price figured?

By heated (living) square feet. Garages, porches and patios are included at no extra charge in a standard size. You get a fixed written quote before any work starts, and the price doesn't change unless you add to the scope.

How many changes can I make?

Concept: 2 rounds. Full Construction Set: 3 rounds. Full Set + 3D: 5 rounds. A round is one list of changes we make all at once. Small fixes we got wrong are always free.

How long does it take?

Concept in about 5 business days. Full Construction Set in about 10 to 15 business days after you approve the floor plan. Full Set + 3D in about 15 to 20 business days. The clock pauses while we wait on your answers.

Do I have to meet in person?

No. Everything is done by email, phone or Zoom. You send your ideas, we send drawings, you mark up changes.

What's not included?

Engineering stamps, truss design (your truss supplier does this), HVAC duct and plumbing pipe layouts, surveys, and permit fees. The electrical plan shows where outlets, switches and lights go, not the wiring. We can help you line these up.

Can I build the plan more than once?

Each package includes the right to build the home once. If you're a builder who wants to build it again, ask us for a multi-build license.
