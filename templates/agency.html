FAQ

Common questions

How do I get started?
Click "Start" on a plan to pay securely with Stripe. Prefer to talk first? Book a 20-min fit call or email us your website.
What happens after I pay?
Kickoff is usually about 3 business days after checkout. You get a 10-question form and an access checklist, and week 1 begins.
How much of my time will this take?
A short form, one-pass approvals on key pages and plans, and a monthly yes/no list (5 or fewer). Meetings only when a decision is stuck. Name one person who can reply within 3 business days.
Can I cancel?
Plans are billed monthly. Cancellation terms are written into your SOW before you start; ask on the fit call.
Do you guarantee results?
No. Nobody honest can guarantee rankings, traffic, or revenue. We commit to the listed work and report real numbers every month.
Who owns the website, accounts, and content?
You do. We work in your own website, Google, ad, and email accounts, and you get a handoff folder after the 90-day build.
What's not included?
Ad spend (paid to Google, Meta, etc. directly), ongoing paid-ads management (an add-on), paid link schemes, and anything not in your plan or SOW.
