FAQ

Straight answers

How much does it cost?
Marketing plans are $1,500, $3,500, $7,000 or $14,000 a month. Add-ons are $400–$1,200 a month. AI plans are a one-time setup ($15,000–$50,000+) plus $3,000–$10,000+ a month. The audit is free.
How fast will I see changes?
Starter: first fixes live in 2–3 weeks. Foundation: top fixes in weeks 2–4. Growth Engine: first campaign in weeks 8–10. AI Starter: live in about 6 weeks. Google rankings take months to build, and nobody can guarantee them.
Can I cancel?
Yes. Marketing plans and add-ons are month-to-month: cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. AI plans have a 3-month minimum after go-live, then the same 30 days' notice.
Which plan should I pick?
Starting small: Starter. Want more Google traffic: Foundation. Ready to grow: Growth Engine. Several locations: Enterprise Command. Missing leads or buried in admin: the AI plans. Need just one thing: an add-on. Still not sure? Get the free audit or book a 20-min fit call.
Is the audit really free?
Yes. Email your business name, website and city. No call and no obligation.
How do I get started?
Foundation, Growth Engine and Enterprise Command: click Start to pay on Stripe; kickoff is about 3 business days later. Starter, add-ons and AI plans: click the button to email us. Prefer to talk first? Book a 20-min fit call.
How much of my time will it take?
Marketing: a short form, one round of approvals and a monthly yes/no list. AI: about 4–6 hours total for AI Starter, more for larger AI plans.
Do you guarantee results?
No. Nobody honest can guarantee rankings, traffic or revenue. We commit to the listed work and report real numbers every month.
Who owns the website, accounts and content?
You do. We work in your accounts, and anything we set up is in your name.
What's not included?
Ad spend (paid to Google, Meta, etc. directly), ongoing ads management, paid links, and anything not in your written scope. AI plans don't include custom apps, replacing your CRM or accounting system, data moves, hardware or compliance certifications unless added.
Why no testimonials?
ApexForge is new, and we won't fake reviews or case studies. We're taking our first 5 clients so each one gets close attention. Prices and terms are exactly as listed.
