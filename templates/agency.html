  • Up to 10 priority pages rewritten and optimized

  • AI brand-voice and claim rules, and a content SOP (brief, AI draft, human edit, publish)
  • 2 new articles or pages per month once the content system is live (first batch weeks 7–8)
  • Positioning one-pager (who, offer, proof, CTA) and a funnel map with the biggest leaks fixed
  • Copy for 1 primary landing page / CTA path
  • Welcome sequence and 1 nurture sequence, if you use email or SMS
  • Monthly async scorecard (real data only), shipped list, and 5 or fewer yes/no decisions

  1. Week 1Short intake, access, baselines (Search Console, Analytics, rankings, primary conversion), site crawl
  2. Weeks 2–4Audit pack and fix list, technical fixes, tracking, brand-voice rules, positioning. You approve top fixes, brand rules, and positioning.
  3. Month 2 (weeks 5–8)Keyword/page map, priority page rewrites, content SOP, first content batch, CTA path fixes. You approve page copy in one pass.
  4. Month 3 (weeks 9–12)Email sequences, Business Profile cleanup, QA crawl and form tests, 90-day scorecard against baselines, handoff folder
  5. Every month after2 new articles or pages, on-page and technical upkeep, scorecard, short yes/no list

What you provide

A 10-question intake ("unknown" is fine); access to your website/CMS, Search Console, Analytics, Google Business Profile, and email or CRM tool; real proof only (reviews, testimonials, logos); any claims you can't make; one approver who answers yes/no within 3 business days.
