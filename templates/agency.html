FAQ

Straight answers

Is the audit really free?
Yes. Email us your business name, website and city and we'll send a short video or report. No call required and no obligation to buy.
Which plan should I pick?
One location and want to start small: Starter ($1,500/mo) gets your Google Business Profile and 5 most important pages right, and you can upgrade to Foundation anytime. Not enough people find you: Foundation. Ready to grow with content, campaigns and follow-up: Growth Engine. Several locations, brands or sites: Enterprise Command. Losing leads or hours to admin: the AI plans. Still not sure? Get the free audit or book a 20-min fit call and we'll tell you plainly.
How do I get started?
Start with the free audit, or click "Start" on a marketing plan to pay securely with Stripe. AI plans start with a kickoff call (no online checkout). Prefer to talk first? Book a 20-min fit call.
What happens after I pay?
Kickoff is usually about 3 business days after checkout. You get a 10-question form and an access checklist, and week 1 begins.
How much of my time will this take?
Marketing: a short form, one-pass approvals on key pages and plans, and a monthly yes/no list (5 or fewer). Name one person who can reply within 3 business days. AI: about 4–6 hours total for AI Starter, 8–12 hours plus staff training for AI Operations, 15–25 hours (mostly department leads) for AI Command.
Can I cancel?
Yes. Plans are billed monthly and run month-to-month. Cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. (AI retainers start at go-live with a 3-month minimum, then run month-to-month with 30 days' notice.)
Do you guarantee results?
No. Nobody honest can guarantee rankings, traffic, revenue or savings. We commit to the listed work and report real numbers every month.
Who owns the website, accounts and content?
You do. We work in your own website, Google, ad, email and software accounts; anything we set up is in your name, and you get a handoff folder.
What's not included?
Ad spend (paid to Google, Meta, etc. directly), ongoing paid-ads management (an add-on), paid link schemes, and anything not in your plan or SOW. For AI plans, unless added to your scope: custom mobile apps, replacing your CRM or accounting system, data moves between platforms, hardware or phone systems, and compliance certifications.
What does "founding client" mean?
ApexForge is a new agency, and we'd rather say so than fake it: no stock testimonials or made-up case studies here. We're taking on our first 5 clients so each one gets close attention. Prices and terms are exactly as listed on this page.
