Privacy Policy

ROOTCAUSE LLC · Last updated September 14, 2026

This policy describes how ROOTCAUSE LLC (“Root Cause Test,” “we”) handles information when you use www.root-cause-test.com or purchase the Root Cause Bioenergetic Scan.

Who we are

ROOTCAUSE LLC operates this site. Questions: test@root-cause-test.com.

Information we collect

  • Account details you provide (name, email, phone, password hash).
  • Order and payment metadata. Card payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store full card numbers.
  • Hair and saliva samples and related scan files you or our team upload so we can complete your wellness scan and report.

How we use it

We use this information to process Stripe checkout, fulfill your scan, create your wellness report, operate your client dashboard, send transactional email or text, and improve the site. Samples and scan files are used for your wellness scan and report — not to sell a medical diagnosis.

Sharing

We share information with service providers who help us run the business (Stripe for payments; hosting and email/SMS vendors). We do not sell your personal information.

Not medical advice

Reports and site content are educational wellness information. They are not medical advice and are not a medical diagnosis or allergy test.
