  • Handoff pack: workflow diagrams, step-by-step guides (SOPs), list of accounts, 1 recorded walkthrough

FAQ

How do I get started?

What happens after I book?

How much of my time will this take?
About 4–6 hours total for AI Starter, 8–12 hours plus staff training for AI Operations, and 15–25 hours (mostly department leads) for AI Command.
