AI Starter $15,000 setup + $3,000/mo · ~6 weeks · you spend ~4–6 hrs total

Estimated go-live: about 6 weeks after kickoff.

What you get

  • AI readiness audit: tool inventory, process map of your top 10 repetitive tasks, time-cost estimate, prioritized automation list
  • 3–5 automated workflows picked from the audit, for example: lead follow-up (instant email/SMS reply, qualifying questions, CRM entry, 3-touch follow-up); scheduling (booking link, confirmations, reminders, reschedules); invoicing (invoice trigger, payment reminders, overdue alerts to the owner); email triage (auto-label and route, AI draft replies for your review, urgent flags); one workflow specific to your business, like review requests after a job closes
  • 1 customer-facing chatbot on your website and/or SMS: answers only from knowledge you approve, captures leads, books or routes, hands off to a person
  • Automation, AI, and hosting accounts set up in your name, with monthly spend caps and alerts
  • Handoff pack: workflow diagrams, SOPs, account inventory, 1 recorded walkthrough
  • Retainer ($3,000/mo): monitoring, fixes, knowledge updates, 1 new automation or change per month, monthly async report (hours saved, leads handled, errors)

Timeline from kickoff

  1. Week 0Kickoff, access checklist, accounts created in your name
  2. Week 1Audit and your 45–60 min walkthrough. You approve the audit and workflow picks.
  3. Weeks 2–3Build and test workflows 1–5. You approve message templates.
  4. Week 4Chatbot knowledge, scripts, and escalation rules on a test page. You approve bot answers and rules.
  5. Week 5Pilot in limited mode; we fix what you flag
  6. Week 6Go-live, handoff pack, walkthrough. Go-live sign-off; retainer starts.

What you provide

Admin or delegated access to CRM, email, calendar, accounting, website, and phone/SMS by day 3; a company card for vendor accounts (billed to you, no markup); existing SOPs, price sheets, and FAQs (rough is fine); one approver who answers yes/no within 2 business days. Payment: $7,500 at signing, $7,500 at go-live.

AI Operations $30,000 setup + $6,000/mo · ~10 weeks · you spend ~8–12 hrs, plus staff training

Estimated go-live: about 10 weeks after kickoff.

What you get

  • Everything in AI Starter: audit, 3–5 workflows, customer chatbot, handoff pack
  • Department interviews (sales, ops, admin) and a 1-page integration architecture: systems map, data flows, access roles, approval rules
  • Your own bot platform, hosted in accounts you own, with 3 internal assistants: sales (lead research, quote and proposal drafts, follow-up drafts, pipeline nudges); support (answers from your knowledge, reply drafts, ticket logging); admin (scheduling, data entry, AP/AR prep, report pulls)
  • Integrations: CRM (contacts, jobs/deals, notes), email and calendar (Google Workspace or Microsoft 365), accounting (QuickBooks, Xero, or similar; read and draft only, no payments without approval)
  • Guardrails: role-based access, a human-approval queue for money and customer-facing sends, audit log
  • Staff training: 2 live sessions (recorded), a 1-page quick guide per role, pilot with 3–5 staff
  • Retainer ($6,000/mo): monthly tuning from usage data, monitoring, up to 2 new automations or bot skills per month, monthly report plus an optional 30-min review

Timeline from kickoff

  1. Week 0Kickoff, access checklist, accounts in your name
  2. Week 1Audit and 2–3 short department interviews. You approve audit and priorities.
  3. Week 2Integration architecture. You approve architecture and guardrails.
  4. Weeks 3–4Starter workflows 1–5 and the customer chatbot. You approve templates and bot answers.
  5. Week 5Sales assistant and CRM integration
  6. Week 6Support assistant, email and calendar integration; your sales lead tests the sales assistant
  7. Week 7Admin assistant and accounting integration (draft-only on money)
  8. Week 8End-to-end QA, access review, audit log. You approve access and money rules.
  9. Week 92 staff training sessions and a pilot with 3–5 staff
  10. Week 10Go-live, handoff pack, first tuning pass. Go-live sign-off; retainer starts.

What you provide

Everything listed for AI Starter, plus one contact per department for a 30-min interview and pilot testing, and staff time for two 45-min trainings. Payment: $15,000 at signing, $15,000 at go-live.
