Bioenergetic wellness scan vs food allergy testing

These two services answer different questions. A Root Cause Bioenergetic Scan is an at-home wellness report from hair and saliva. Clinical food allergy testing is medical care. This page exists so those are not confused.

Root Cause bioenergetic scan Clinical food allergy testing
Purpose Wellness report with priority themes and optional ideas Evaluate possible allergic response under clinical care
Sample Hair and saliva collected at home, then shipped Set by the clinician (often blood, skin, or supervised challenge)
Outputs Written wellness report in a client dashboard Clinical results interpreted by a licensed clinician
Validation / limits Wellness information only; not a diagnostic method Used in medical practice; still requires professional interpretation
When to see a clinician Always for symptoms, suspected allergy, or disease questions This is the clinical path for those questions

Clear limit

A Root Cause scan does not diagnose food allergy, does not rule food allergy out, and is not a substitute for clinical care. Do not use it to decide whether to eat a food that has caused a reaction. Seek a licensed clinician for allergy, anaphylaxis risk, or any disease concern.
