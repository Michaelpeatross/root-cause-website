What drives the price

  • Sample types: hair only, saliva only, or both.
  • Report depth: some list hundreds of items; others prioritize. More items is not always more useful.
  • Practitioner time: scans that include a consultation usually cost more.
  • Shipping and kits: some include a prepaid kit in the price; others charge for it.

Hidden costs to check before you buy

  • Is return shipping included, or do you pay for a kit?
  • Are retests or follow-up scans discounted?
  • What is the refund policy if you have not mailed your samples yet?
  • Are supplements or follow-up products pushed after the report?

Is a more expensive scan better?

Not necessarily. All of these are wellness tools rather than medical tests, and scientific evidence for bioenergetic methods is limited. The most useful report is one you can actually act on: a short list of priorities you can test with an elimination diet and a food journal.
