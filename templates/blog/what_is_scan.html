What is a bioenergetic hair and saliva wellness scan?

Root Cause Test offers an at-home bioenergetic hair and saliva wellness scan for $199. You collect samples at home, ship them, and receive a prioritized written wellness report. This article explains the service in plain language.

What it is

It is a wellness scan that uses hair and saliva samples you mail in. The output is a report organized by body-system themes, with priority notes and optional supplement or lifestyle ideas. Collection does not require a clinic or a blood draw.

What it is not

  • Not a medical diagnosis or treatment plan
  • Not an allergy test and not a way to detect or rule out disease
  • Not a DNA test
  • Not HTMA (hair tissue mineral analysis from a clinical laboratory)
  • Not a substitute for licensed medical care

Careful comparison

Clinical allergy testing is ordered and interpreted in a medical setting to evaluate allergic response. A Root Cause scan does not do that work and must not be used to decide whether a food or substance is “safe.”

HTMA is a laboratory measurement of mineral content in hair. A Root Cause bioenergetic scan is a different method and a different kind of report. They are not interchangeable.

See also: Bioenergetic scan vs food allergy test.

Who may be interested

People who want an at-home wellness report from hair and saliva, and who already work with — or are willing to see — a licensed clinician for symptoms, allergies, or disease questions.

Samples and timing

Collect hair and saliva as instructed, then mail them using the prepaid label and kit directions we send after your order. Typical turnaround is 7–14 days after samples arrive. Details: How it works.
