Open cell vs closed cell

Two kinds of foam. We'll match each area to the right one.

Both seal air leaks that regular insulation leaves behind. The difference is how dense the foam is and what it's best at.

Open-cell spray foam

Soft, lightweight foam that expands to fill every gap.

Why people choose it

  • Seals air leaks around framing, wiring and odd-shaped spaces
  • Quiets a room: soaks up sound between rooms and floors
  • Usually the more budget-friendly way to fill big areas
  • Flexible, so it moves with the house as it settles

Best for

  • Attics and rooflines
  • Interior and exterior walls
  • Sound rooms, media rooms and bedrooms

Closed-cell spray foam

Dense, rigid foam that blocks air and moisture.

Why people choose it

  • More insulation per inch, great where space is tight
  • Resists moisture, a big deal in south Louisiana humidity
  • Adds stiffness to the walls, floors and roof deck it's sprayed on
  • Tough enough for garages and under-floor areas

Best for

  • Floors, subfloors and under the house
  • Garages and shops
  • Around pipes and other spots exposed to damp air

Not sure which you need? That's normal. Many homes use both, such as open-cell in the attic and closed-cell under the floor. We'll walk through it on the free quote visit.

Common questions

How much does spray foam insulation cost?
Every job is different, so we price after a free on-site measure. Cost depends on the square footage, open-cell vs closed-cell, and how thick the foam needs to be. Call (337) 849-3132 for a free quote.
Should I use open-cell or closed-cell?
Open-cell is great for attics, rooflines, walls and sound control. Closed-cell is denser, blocks moisture, and is a strong pick for floors, subfloors, garages and pipes. Many homes use both. We'll recommend what fits each area during the quote.
Why spray foam instead of regular insulation?
Spray foam expands to fill gaps and cracks, so it stops air leaks as well as insulating. In humid south Louisiana, that means less muggy outside air getting in and a house that's easier to keep cool.
