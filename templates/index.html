FAQ

Is this a medical test?

No. Root Cause Test is a wellness scan. It is not a medical diagnosis, not an allergy test, not a DNA test, and not intended to detect or treat disease.

How is this different from bloodwork?

Bloodwork is a clinical lab service ordered with a licensed clinician. This is an at-home bioenergetic hair and saliva wellness scan that produces a prioritized wellness report and supplement ideas. It does not replace bloodwork or a doctor visit.

How do I ship my samples?

Mail free in a regular envelope with a postage stamp, choose the optional $15 prepaid collection kit, or overnight the envelope for the fastest turnaround. The $15 kit is convenience only — it is not required.

How long until I get my report?

Typical turnaround is 7–14 days after your hair and saliva samples arrive. For the fastest turnaround, overnight the envelope to the collection site.

Will this replace my doctor?

No. Do not use this scan instead of licensed medical care. Share questions about symptoms or treatment with your clinician.

How much does it cost?

The Root Cause Bioenergetic Scan is $199, paid securely through Stripe checkout. Standard letter mail is free. An optional prepaid collection kit is $15 if you want that convenience — it is not required.
