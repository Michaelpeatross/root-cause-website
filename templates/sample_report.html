What a Root Cause wellness report looks like

After hair and saliva samples are processed, the written report is meant to be easy to scan — not a raw marker dump. This page explains the layout. It is a description of the report format, not your personal results and not medical advice.

Your top 3 priorities

The report opens with a numbered list — Your top 3 priorities — written in everyday language. Color badges mark relative emphasis as High, Medium, or Low. Those badges are informational only. Each item includes a simple first step. Lower-priority notes stay available further down and are not the lead.

Body-system sections

Findings are grouped by body system or theme (for example energy, digestion, skin, sleep, or stress-related patterns). Each system also carries a High / Medium / Low badge next to its Health Score so you can see relative emphasis at a glance.

Optional supplement and lifestyle ideas

Some reports include educational product or lifestyle ideas tied to those themes. They are suggestions for discussion, not prescriptions, dosing instructions, or a treatment plan.

What this report is not

A Root Cause report is informational wellness content. It is not a medical diagnosis, not treatment, not an allergy test, not a DNA test, not HTMA, and not a tool for detecting or ruling out disease. It does not replace a licensed clinician.
