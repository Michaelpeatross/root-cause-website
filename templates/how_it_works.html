How the at-home scan works

Four steps. No clinic visit. $199 for one Root Cause Bioenergetic Scan.

1. Order

Pay $199 on the secure Stripe checkout. You do not need an account to pay. Create a free account after purchase so collection instructions and your dashboard are ready.

2. Collect hair + saliva

Use the instructions in your portal. Take hair from a clean, product-light area and collect saliva as directed. Do not send extra items that are not requested. Keep samples labeled with the name that matches your order.

3. Ship

Mail your samples using the prepaid label and kit directions we send after your order. Typical reporting time starts when the package arrives — not on the day you check out.

4. Dashboard report

When processing finishes, the prioritized wellness report appears in your client portal. You can read it there and download a PDF when that option is available.

What to expect

  • Turnaround is typically 7–14 days after samples arrive.
  • The report is wellness information, not a diagnosis.
  • Questions: test@root-cause-test.com or Contact.
